Good to know

Common questions

How do I choose a skincare routine?

Begin with a gentle cleanser, moisturizer, and daily broad-spectrum sunscreen. Additional products should reflect your skin's needs and tolerance. Ask us about individualized guidance.

Can I ask about available treatments?

Yes. Send us a message with your goals or questions, and we can follow up with information about current offerings.

Are red light therapy devices right for everyone?

Not necessarily. Check the manufacturer's contraindications, especially if you take photosensitizing medication or have a medical or eye condition. Consult a qualified clinician if uncertain.
